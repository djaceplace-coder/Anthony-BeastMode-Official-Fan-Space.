# Data Requests Before Public Launch — Anthony BeastMode Management

Before the public announcement and marketing launch of this website, the management and tour coordination team must verify and supply the following concrete data records.

---

### 1. Upcoming Shows & Ticketing Verification (Rule 1.10 & 6.4)
For every scheduled live concert date, provide:
1. **Official Ticketing URL**: Must link to the verified venue box office or official vendor (e.g. Eventbrite, TicketLeap, Bandsintown).
2. **Ticketing Vendor Name**: Exact company name for legal attribution.
3. **Price Label**: Exact advance price (e.g. "$25 advance / $30 at doors").
4. **Price Verification Date**: Timestamped date of verification (must be within 14 days of publication).
5. **Age Admission Policy**: Sign-off on `All Ages`, `18+ Only`, or `21+ Only` determined by venue ordinance.

---

### 2. City Strategy Metrics (Section 2.5)
For the 11 launch cities (Tier 1: Blue Springs MO, Kansas City MO, St. Louis MO, Dayton OH, Battle Creek MI, Evansville IN, Memphis TN; Tier 2: Thomas WV, Englewood CO, Arlington WA; Tier 3: San Diego CA), provide:
1. **Spotify Monthly Listeners**: Current 28-day listener count extracted from Spotify for Artists for that metropolitan area.
2. **Historical Sell-Through**: Ticket counts sold on previous routing stops in or adjacent to that market.
3. **Social Follower Geo-Breakdown**: Instagram/TikTok audience percentages per region.

---

### 3. Platform Streaming Catalog Links (Section 5.1 & 5.2)
1. **Verified Artist Profile IDs**:
   - Spotify Artist URI
   - Apple Music Artist URL
   - YouTube Music Channel URL
   - Deezer Artist Link
   - Bandcamp Artist Store
2. **Official Release Embed URIs**: Official embed iframes for *Trailer Park Baby*, *Outlaw Country Roads*, and future announcements.

---

### 4. Explicit Content & Lyrics Licensing Flags (Rule 1.4 & 1.6)
1. **Per-Track Explicit Flags**: Confirm `explicit: true` vs. `explicit: false` for all catalog tracks.
2. **Lyrics Licensing Status**: Confirm `not_shown` (default), `licensed` (with verified publisher license), or `instrumental`.

---

### 5. Partner Offers Compliance (Section 7)
For any active partner offer (e.g., Razer Gold, Apple Music, Local Venues):
1. **Signed Terms Agreement Reference Number** (e.g., `AGR-2026-X`).
2. **Complete Unedited Legal Fine Print** including eligibility, expiration, and exclusions.
3. **Ticketing Partner Written Approval** for any ticket-related promotional perks.
